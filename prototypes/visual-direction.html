Bodha — Visual Direction

Three directions over the same Home content, anchored to bodhalauncher.png. Flip with the bar below or ←/→ keys.

Direction A

Manuscript

The anchor image, committed to: warm paper ground, a high-contrast serif voice for the clock and headings, a quiet humanist sans for interface text, soft outlined cards, sage used sparingly. Feels like a well-made notebook.
