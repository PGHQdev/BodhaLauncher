# Pricing: $3/mo · $20/yr (7-day trial) · $60 lifetime, whole figures

Bodha Pro prices at the top of the launcher band ($10–20/yr market), not the blocker band ($30–60/yr): launcher-comparison shoppers anchor there, free launchers (Olauncher) bite in that frame, and "half of Jomo" stays a defensible value story. Whole figures, no .99 charm pricing — the billing spec's no-urgency-manipulation stance made visible at the purchase moment; Play price templates handle regional equivalents. Structure: monthly $3 (the low-commitment entry; $36/yr held all year makes annual's ~45% saving real, not staged), annual $20 with a 7-day free trial (the only trial — monthly is its own trial, lifetime can't trial), lifetime $60 non-consumable at the standard 3× multiple so annual stays the default buy and revenue stays recurring. No intro-offer discounts. All three unlock the single `bodha_pro` entitlement. Alternatives rejected: blocker-band pricing, .99 pricing, no-monthly (Niagara-style), lifetime at $50 (2.5× tilts buyers off recurring revenue and launch price is the floor forever). Market survey: `docs/research/pricing-competitors.md` on branch `research/pricing` (issue #37). Boundary: ADR 0005. Resolved in issue #38.
